## 🎯 AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) Strategies

### Featured Snippet Optimization

**Question Type: "What is..."**

- Position feature in FAQ #1
- Include clear definition (max 40-50 words)
- Follow with 2-3 examples
- Add visual representation (table/comparison)
  ✓ Target: Google Featured Snippet + Perplexity

**Question Type: "How to..."**

- Provide step-by-step guide (max 5 steps)
- Use clear headings (Step 1, Step 2...)
- Include image/diagram for each step
- Format in table or list for easy scanning
  ✓ Target: "How it Works" section

**Question Type: "How much / How many..."**

- Lead with specific number/amount
- Real example with calculations
- Range if varies by condition
  ✓ Target: "How much can I save" FAQ

### Semantic Keywords (NLP-optimized)

- **Synonyms used:** loan merging, combining debts, debt unification, consolidation
- **Related terms:** EMI reduction, interest rate, credit score improvement, financial planning
- **Long-form variations:** "How to consolidate multiple loans into one", "Should I consolidate credit card debt", "Best way to reduce EMI"

### Voice Search Optimization

- Added conversational tone throughout
- Used question-based headings
- Included featured snippet-friendly structured answers
- Added directional language: "Here's how...", "Let me explain..."
